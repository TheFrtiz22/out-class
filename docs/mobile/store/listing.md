## Description

OutClass is where students find campus clubs and where clubs run recruiting.

For students
- Explore every club at your school and save the ones you like.
- Apply once with your profile, and pick up a draft where you left it.
- Book and reschedule interviews, and add them to your calendar.
- See decisions and respond to offers.
- Check in to meetings and events by scanning a code, or show your pass at the door.
- Keep up with announcements, tasks and your calendar.

For club leaders
- Run check-in from your phone: show the room's code, or scan each member's pass.
- Host a live vote and see the result as it closes.
- Glance at your pipeline and open the full review on the web.
- Send an announcement to members or applicants.

OutClass starts at the University of Virginia. You sign in with your university email.
OutClass is not affiliated with or endorsed by the University of Virginia.
