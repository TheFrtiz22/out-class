About OutClass

Club recruitment, without the scattered forms and spreadsheets.

OutClass is being built by UVA students to make it easier to discover organizations, apply, schedule interviews, and keep track of what happens next.

Create your profile Why we're building it

Why this exists

Club recruitment is scattered.

Students juggle separate forms, email threads, deadlines, and interview links. Club leaders juggle spreadsheets, scheduling, reviewer notes, and decisions.

That work is already complicated enough. We're building one place to handle it, without asking clubs to give up control of their recruiting process.

The product

One process. Two workspaces.

For students

Find clubs. Apply. See what's next.

Discover organizations. Apply through one profile. Manage interviews and follow your recruitment status.

See the student side

For club leaders

Run recruitment with your team.

Configure applications, coordinate interviews, collaborate on evaluations, and manage recruiting decisions.

See the leader side

The team behind it

Built by students at UVA.

OutClass started as a student-built project at the University of Virginia. We're beginning with selective and application-based organizations because their recruitment involves real coordination on both sides.

We're rolling out with participating clubs one at a time. That means testing the actual workflow, listening to the students and leaders using it, and fixing what doesn't work before taking it to other campuses.
