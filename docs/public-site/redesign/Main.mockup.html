Does OutClass cost anything?

It's free for students. Clubs join the campus pilot individually.

Who can sign up?

Students and club leaders sign in with their UVA email.

How does my club get access?

Request your club's workspace. Claim requests are reviewed before access is granted.

Is OutClass run by the University?

No. OutClass is operated by OutClassPlatforms LLC and is not an official University of Virginia service.
