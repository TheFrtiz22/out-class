## Agreed product contract

1. **Applicant panel:** name, headshot, scholar status, and résumé thumbnail only. Remove GPA, major, SAT/ACT and application responses from this panel without deleting their underlying data or unrelated uses. Other existing panel content, such as observations and evaluation feedback, also stays outside this compact panel.
2. **Scholar status:** belongs in personal-profile Education, below graduation year and above GPA. Multiple selections: Jefferson Scholar, Walentas Scholar, Echols Scholar, Rodman Scholar, Not Applicable, Other. Not Applicable is exclusive; Other requires text. Unanswered legacy profiles remain unanswered. Display selected scholarship names below the headshot and above the résumé; omit a badge for Not Applicable. Do not equate unanswered with Not Applicable.
3. **Round master kit:** every round has an Interview master kit. Presidents, vice-presidents and board members can edit it through explicit server-enforced permissions. Assigned interviewers can read/use questions without editing the master kit.
4. **Workspace:** three desktop columns: compact applicant panel left, wider question workspace middle, completed questions right. A question-bank control opens the bank inside the workspace. Selecting a question enlarges it in the middle with “Your private question notes.” Preserve “Add an off-script question.”
5. **Question completion:** “Save and close” saves notes and marks that question complete for this interviewer only, highlights it, and moves it to their completed column. Completion never makes private notes shared. Only confirm completion/movement after a successful save; retain edits and allow retry on conflict/failure.
6. **Résumé overlay:** clicking the thumbnail opens a large overlay; closing restores the thumbnail workspace. Support text highlights with anchored comments and a general résumé-notes icon on the right edge. No freehand drawing.
7. **Annotation access:** visible to the currently assigned panel, never the applicant. Every annotation displays its author. Only its author or this club's president/vice-president can edit/delete it. President/vice-president moderation access is allowed. Board membership alone confers no résumé-annotation access; a board member may access annotations independently through a valid panel assignment.
8. **Version/access preservation:** preserve the résumé version used during the interview. Later uploads cannot move highlights to a replacement document. Former panel members lose relevant access when revoked; historical assignment or authorship alone cannot retain access.
9. **End interview:** opens an editable closing review, not a submission. Interviewers discuss their thoughts before independently selecting scores. Do not synchronize another interviewer's draft score or prefill a consensus score.
10. **Closing fields:** “Questions the applicant asked,” “Additional notes,” and “Overall score.” Slider only, 1–10 in 0.5 steps. Start unscored, require deliberate selection, and display the chosen value. A visual initial thumb position must not silently assign a score.
11. **Calibration history:** above the slider show read-only applicant names and scores for this interviewer's last five completed interviews in the same club and round, excluding the current interview. Return fewer when fewer eligible records exist; never pad with another interviewer's reviews.
12. **Submission:** “Save and finish” submits only that interviewer's review. Submitted closing notes/scores are immutable through every application write path. Presidents, vice-presidents and board members can reopen submitted reviews for read-only viewing. Reopening never unlocks edits.
13. **Anonymity:** preserve existing anonymous recruiting-decision labels and server-side privacy protections. No identifying résumé, scholarship, free-text or score-history data may enter anonymous views or responses. The ordinary authorized interview room remains identified. Do not disable a round's existing privacy setting to achieve this layout: deny identified-room data when the applicable privacy policy forbids it and retain the anonymous workflow separately.
14. **Separate visibility:** private question notes, panel résumé annotations, and leader-visible closing reviews are distinct categories with distinct server projections. Neither question completion nor leader status grants access to another interviewer's private notes.
15. **Demo Mode:** remains isolated from production writes, including uploads, annotations, role assignment, question completion and review submission.

## Proposed authorization and data design (not implemented)

### Explicit office mapping

Introduce a persisted club-scoped `ClubOfficeAssignment` with membership FK, office enum PRESIDENT/VICE_PRESIDENT/BOARD, grantor, grantedAt and revokedAt, plus audited grant/revoke operations. Allow multiple offices explicitly where needed. Default existing members to no office; do not backfill from `title`, legacy `role`, accessRole, groups, isOwner, email or display text.

An active verified club owner can explicitly attest initial office assignments through a dedicated server action using the existing ownership/access-management checks, with authenticated actor identity and append-only audit. This is assignment authority, not automatic content access. Ordinary `leaders.manage` alone must not grant authority to self-appoint or manufacture a higher office. Future delegation of office-assignment authority needs its own persisted, audited authorization. Actual office holders must be confirmed by the club; this stage assigns nobody.

Use explicit derived capabilities such as `interview.kit.edit`, `interview.closing.read` and `interview.resume.moderate` from current office assignments. PRESIDENT/VICE_PRESIDENT/BOARD get the first two; only PRESIDENT/VICE_PRESIDENT get moderation. Do not feed these sensitive checks through the current blanket `isOwner => all permissions` shortcut. Owner/admin/recruiter status alone must not bypass panel, note or annotation privacy. Disabled users, inactive memberships and revoked office assignments fail closed.

| Data/action | Assigned interviewer | President / VP | Board without panel assignment | Applicant |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Read/use master questions | Yes, relevant club/round | Yes | Yes | No |
| Edit master kit | Only with explicit qualifying office | Yes | Yes | No |

| Other interviewers' question notes | No | No | No | No |
| Interview résumé/annotations | Yes while access remains valid | Yes for moderation | No | No interview annotation access |
| Edit/delete annotation | Own only, with current access | Yes, audited moderation | No | No |
| Draft closing review | Own only | Own only, if assigned | No | No |

| Named five-interview history | Own authorized history only | Own history if interviewing | No special history grant | No |

Applicants retain normal access to their own current résumé/profile through the existing self-service flow, not access to interview annotations. All table entries are subject to club/round scoping and privacy policy. A revoked panel author can regain a distinct moderation right only through a valid current president/VP assignment, never from historical authorship.

### Persistence and concurrency

- **Scholarship:** nullable structured field with schema version, enum selections and bounded trimmed Other text, or equivalent normalized columns. `null` means unanswered; reject duplicates, unknown options, Not Applicable combined with anything, and Other without text. Clear stale Other text when Other is deselected. Preserve GPA/major/test scores. Treat Other text as identifying free text in all projections.
- **Assignment:** introduce an explicit revocable panel-assignment relation scoped to club/application/round (and booking where applicable), with membership FK and audit provenance. Link known room assignments through a reviewed deterministic migration or authorized scheduling operation; ambiguous/legacy cases stay unassigned. Recheck active assignment and membership on every session, file, annotation and history request, including transaction-time writes. Application progression must not accidentally expose or erase historical records; authorize historical reads by their recorded round and current relevant access.
- **Questions:** extend versioned InterviewRecord draft with durable completion state/timestamp for each snapshotted or off-script question ID. Default legacy questions to incomplete, not inferred from nonempty notes. Keep completed notes private and scoped to the record's interviewer. Save notes and completion atomically using revision checks; reject foreign IDs and stale writes. Question reopening for the owner may allow correction before final submission; must not reopen a submitted closing review. Keep master-kit edits separate from individual session snapshots.
- **Closing review:** separate explicitly projected closing fields from question notes, even if a compatible draft envelope is retained. Prefer a dedicated final review keyed by application/interviewer/roundId, containing applicantQuestions, additionalNotes, score, submittedAt and immutable provenance. Use the existing Evaluation only as a controlled downstream projection or migrate to a single canonical final-review source; never keep two independently writable truths.
- **Finalization:** atomic compare-and-set from draft to submitted, with revision check and audit. Require finite score in [1,10] and half-point increments on server and database. Retry is idempotent for the same submission; competing or subsequent changes fail. Route legacy `submitEvaluation`, session completion, demo adapters and any nested/admin mutations through the same guard. Add database protection for submitted review UPDATE/DELETE and protected linked Evaluation mutations, including unlink/reset bypasses. Do not permit a leader reopening endpoint to clear submittedAt/completedAt. Scope retention/deletion policies separately; application writes cannot silently cascade away final reviews.
- **Legacy finals:** preserve historical scores and notes as recorded; do not round old decimals, invent applicant questions, or infer ambiguous round IDs. Map old overallReview to Additional notes only when provenance is unambiguous; retain original snapshot/canonical source for discrepancies and manual review. Never silently rewrite or unlock existing submitted records to fit the new schema.
- **History:** server derives current interviewer identity and club/roundId; filter submitted records, exclude current interview, apply current access and anonymity rules, order by submittedAt descending then stable ID, take five. Return only name and final score (plus minimal internal key if needed), not other reviewers' notes or profiles. Round name edits must not alter grouping. Historical assignment alone is not access. Exclude inaccessible records and clearly present an empty state when necessary.

### Résumé versioning and annotation boundaries

Create an immutable résumé version reference pinned once per application/round interview context, shared by that panel's interview records. Record private object key, content hash, size/MIME, original upload identity and capture time. Snapshot creation must be race-safe so simultaneous panel opens use the same document. A replacement upload produces a new version and cannot mutate/delete an object referenced by an interview. Do not use a mutable external URL or expiring signed URL as the version identifier. Legacy external documents require controlled server-side ingestion with URL/size/type checks and SSRF protection, or an explicit unavailable state; do not silently annotate the live external link.

Use a dedicated annotation entity keyed to that interview context and résumé version, with immutable author membership/user identity, kind (TEXT_HIGHLIGHT or GENERAL_NOTE), comment, revision and timestamps. Text anchors include page, normalized text offsets, exact quote with prefix/suffix and version-specific geometry. Validate bounds against the pinned document. Resolve/re-render across zoom without relocating onto another version. Scanned/image-only PDFs need an explicit unsupported-text-selection state; general notes still work. OCR is an optional later dependency, not a reason to invent anchors. Preserve author attribution on moderation, record moderator separately, and use revision checks for concurrent edits/deletes. Render comments as text, not HTML.

Serve the interview thumbnail and PDF through an authenticated, no-store, assignment-aware proxy, including range requests, instead of handing out reusable five-minute URLs. Reauthorize annotation polling/mutations and clear client state on revocation. Reject cross-club/round/version IDs and direct object-path guesses. Existing loaded/downloaded bytes cannot be recalled from a former viewer; the enforceable guarantee is no subsequent server access, no reusable storage capability, and removal of data from the active UI on access failure. Preserve private storage and browser-role denial. Choose a PDF text-layer renderer only during implementation after checking accessibility, worker/CSP compatibility, license and bundle cost; no new dependency is installed in this stage.

### UX and data isolation

Reuse current dialog, slider, autosave and revision machinery. Desktop middle column is widest; responsive layouts preserve reading order and make completed questions/bank reachable without horizontal overflow. The résumé overlay traps focus, supports Escape and restores focus to the thumbnail; general-note control has a visible/accessible label. “Save and close” and “Save and finish” show pending/error states and prevent duplicate saves. Keyboard slider interaction counts as deliberate selection; simply opening the review does not. “End interview” may save a draft but cannot finalize it, including via Ctrl/Cmd+Enter. Closing review remains editable until the explicit final action.

Keep a narrowly projected private-note endpoint, a panel-annotation endpoint and a submitted-review endpoint. Do not serialize an entire InterviewRecord into leader review screens. Do not reuse global shared applicant display settings to reintroduce forbidden left-panel fields. Anonymous decision APIs must enforce omissions server-side even when clients request identified fields. Demo adapters must use local fixtures for résumé bytes/thumbnails and never reach production storage or real server actions; test both enabled-demo and ordinary-live routing.
